# Remote Work and Hybrid Policy

(Fictional policy, created only for testing this project.)

## Hybrid Model
AI follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office 3 days a week and may work from home 2 days a week. The default office days are Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Teams can agree on different days with the approval of the department head.

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation are eligible for work from home. Employees on probation work from the office all five days so that they can learn from their teams.

## Work From Home Requirements
- A stable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps.
- Availability on Microsoft Teams during working hours, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
- Cameras are expected to be on during client calls.
- Company laptops must be used for all work. Personal laptops are not allowed to access client data.

## Work From Home Allowance
Eligible employees receive a monthly work-from-home allowance of Rs. 1,500 for internet and electricity, paid along with the salary.

## Working From Another City
Working from another city or from your hometown for more than 5 consecutive days needs written approval from your manager and HR. Working from outside India is not permitted without approval from the CTO and the Finance team because of tax and data-security rules.

## Security
Always connect to the company VPN before opening internal systems. Do not work from public Wi-Fi networks in cafes or airports without the VPN. Lock your laptop whenever you step away.
