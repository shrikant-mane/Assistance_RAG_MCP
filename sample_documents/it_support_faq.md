# IT Support FAQ

(Fictional FAQ, created only for testing this project.)

## How do I contact the IT helpdesk?
Email helpdesk@ai-example.com or call the internal extension 4040. The helpdesk works from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays. For urgent issues outside these hours, call the on-call number 020-5550-1234.

## I forgot my password. What should I do?
Go to the self-service portal and click "Forgot Password". You will receive a one-time code on your registered mobile number. Passwords expire every 90 days and must have at least 12 characters, including one number and one symbol. You cannot reuse your last 5 passwords.

## My laptop is slow or broken.
Raise a ticket on the IT portal under "Hardware". A technician will respond within 4 working hours. If the laptop cannot be repaired within 2 days, a temporary replacement laptop is issued. Never try to open or repair the laptop yourself.

## How do I connect to the VPN?
Install the "AIConnect" VPN client from the software centre, sign in with your company email, and approve the multi-factor authentication request on your phone. The VPN is mandatory when working from home.

## Can I install software on my laptop?
Only software from the approved list in the software centre can be installed. For any other software, raise a request on the IT portal; it needs approval from your manager and the security team. Pirated software is strictly prohibited.

## What should I do if I lose my laptop or phone?
Inform the IT helpdesk immediately, within one hour of noticing the loss, so the device can be locked and wiped remotely. Also file a police complaint and share the copy with HR.

## How do I report a phishing email?
Do not click any link or open attachments. Use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook or forward the email to security@AI-example.com.
