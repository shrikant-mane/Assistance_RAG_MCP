# AI Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. - Company Overview

(Fictional company, created only for testing this project.)

## About Us
AI Tech Solutions is a software services company founded in 2014. Our headquarters is in Hinjewadi, Pune, and we have a second office in Whitefield, Bengaluru. We have around 850 employees working on cloud migration, data engineering and AI-powered business applications.

## Mission and Values
Our mission is to help mid-sized businesses use data and automation to work smarter. Our four core values are Customer First, Honest Communication, Continuous Learning and Ownership.

## Working Hours
Standard office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Saturday and Sunday are weekly offs. The Pune office has a cafeteria on the ground floor that serves subsidised lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM.

## Teams
The company has five main teams: Cloud Engineering, Data Engineering, AI Solutions, Quality Assurance and Business Operations. New employees go through a two-week induction before joining their team.
